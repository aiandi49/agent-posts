The hour nobody bills for

Arithmetic, not a study: 20 × 3 = 60 minutes a day, 365 days a year. Swap in your own numbers and the shape holds.

The eight skills

Each one takes a named input, returns a named output, and hands off without asking. Open any card for the contract.

How a comment becomes a message

The funnel is the only skill that keeps working after the post goes up. It reads the transcript for the word you already said out loud, then watches for it.

Viewer comments the trigger word, typed under the post
Keyword matches pulled from the transcript before the post went live
Direct message sends with the link, within seconds
  1. 01 The caption skill reads the transcript and proposes the one word worth replying to.
  2. 02 That word gets registered against the post before it publishes, not after.
  3. 03 Every matching comment gets the reply and the message. You never open the app.

Four slots, claimed in order

The uploader does not ask when to post. It takes the next open slot in the day and fills it, which is what makes three posts a day survivable.

Slots are a starting grid, not a rule. Change them once, in the schedule file, and every later run obeys.
SlotWhat it is forIf the slot is already taken
3:00 AM The overnight drop, so the morning scroll finds something new. Falls through to 6:00 AM.
6:00 AM Commute traffic. Short, hooky, no setup required. Falls through to 9:00 AM.
9:00 AM The teaching post. Longest watch time of the four. Falls through to 12:00 PM.
12:00 PM Lunch. Best slot for anything that ends in a question. Queues for 3:00 AM the next day.

What it takes to wire this up

Three moving parts, and one of them is just a folder. Nothing here is exotic; the work is in the handoffs, not the components.

A place to drop files

A watched folder is enough to start. Dropping a render in is the whole trigger.

  • One folder per channel
  • Filename carries nothing; metadata comes from the transcript
  • Keep the original until the post confirms

A model that listens and writes

Speech to text first, then a second pass that turns the transcript into captions, keyword and cover brief.

  • Transcription runs locally or hosted
  • Captions get a length limit per platform
  • The keyword must appear in the spoken audio

A publisher with credentials

Each destination needs its own posting access and its own retry rules. This is where runs actually fail.

  • Store tokens outside the repo
  • Log every attempt with the slot it claimed
  • Fail loudly; a silent skip looks like success
Worth saying plainly This page is a build spec written from a workflow demo, not from vendor documentation. Two things to check against current docs before you commit to them:
  • Posting access and automated-publishing rules differ by platform and change often. Confirm what each destination currently allows for scheduled and API-driven posts.
  • Automatic replies and direct messages triggered by comments are governed by each platform's own automation policy. Confirm the current limits before you turn the funnel on.
Everything else here — the eight skills, the order, the slots — is the workflow itself, and it is ours to change.

Build it once, then stop thinking about it

The point was never the automation. It was getting the hour back, three times a day, without the posts getting worse.
